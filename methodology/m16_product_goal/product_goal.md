# Product goal

The task says what to build. The goal says what the result is for and who reads it, and it stands
one line above the task.

The goal is not a second task. It settles what the task leaves open — which reading of an ambiguous
requirement, which of two equally correct shapes, what is worth doing and what is not — and it
settles them in one direction: toward what the reader of the result needs.

**Every change is justified against it.** Your answer lists the changes you made, one line each,
and each line ends with how that change serves the goal. A change you cannot connect to the goal in
one clause was not needed; take it out before you answer rather than explaining it afterwards.

Where the goal and the task disagree, the task decides what is built and the goal decides how far
to go. Say in your answer when that happened, and which way you went.
