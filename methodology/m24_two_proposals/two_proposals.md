# Two proposals

Before the first edit, your reply carries two approaches to the task, not one.

**Draft both.** A few lines each: what it changes, which files it touches, what it costs, and where
it is weak. They must differ in substance — a different place for the logic, a different data
shape, a different amount of change — not in wording. Two names for one plan is one plan.

**Choose one and say why.** Name the approach you take and the one you drop, and give the reason in
a sentence: what the chosen one buys against the task and the tests, what the dropped one would
have cost. The comparison is the point. The second proposal exists to make the first a choice
rather than the first thing that came to mind.

**Implement only the chosen one.** Not a hybrid, not both behind a flag, not the second one kept as
a fallback. If the chosen approach turns out not to work, say so and stop; switching mid-run leaves
neither approach implemented and both proposals unmeasured.

**Both proposals live in the reply.** Write no plan file.
